BIOGRAPHY

THE FOUNDATION

Sando is a UK producer defined by high-pressure energy and a refusal to be pinned down to a single sound. Originally emerging through the UK Bass music scene, his current focus has shifted into the world of Hard Dance and Bounce, bringing a fresh, evolving perspective to the Northwest’s electronic heritage.

THE IMPACT

Since starting in 2020, Sando has bypassed trends in favor of a distinct, unpredictable style. This approach has earned support from DJ EZ, Dr Fresch, and BBC Radio 1, alongside a Beatport #1 for his single 'Really Tight' and multiple Spotify editorial playlist adds.

THE TRAJECTORY

With a history of sets, including at Ministry of Sound and a portfolio of releases on 3000 Bass and Yosh Bass, Sando is now focused on the intersection of local culture and festival-scale production. Whether it’s a heavy remix or an original production, the goal remains the same: taking raw, energetic music from Ellesmere Port to the biggest stages possible.
