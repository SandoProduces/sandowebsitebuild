BIOGRAPHY

THE FOUNDATION

Sando is a UK producer defined by high-pressure energy and a refusal to be pinned down to a single sound. Originally emerging through the UK Bass music scene, his current focus has shifted into the world of Hard Dance and Bounce, bringing a fresh, evolving perspective to the Northwest’s electronic heritage.

THE IMPACT

Since starting in 2021, Sando has bypassed trends in favor of a distinct, unpredictable style. This approach has earned support from DJ EZ and Dr Fresch, alongside a Beatport #1 for his single 'Really Tight' and multiple Spotify editorial playlist adds. More recently, for the new direction in Bounce music, support has been gained by legendary DJ's like Lee Butler and Dave Bolton with multiple prime time radio plays.

THE TRAJECTORY

With a history of sets and a portfolio of releases on 3000 Bass and Yosh Bass, Sando is now focused on the intersection of local culture and festival-scale production. Whether it’s a heavy remix or an original production, the goal remains the same: taking raw, energetic music from Ellesmere Port to the biggest stages possible.
