OUR SOUND

The Treasury Records is an independent electronic music label based in the North West of the UK. Founded by Sando as a dedicated platform for high-energy Hard Dance and UK Bounce, the imprint focuses on forward-thinking production that doesn’t always fit cleanly into standard genre brackets.

Our output is built on creative freedom. Rather than locking into one specific tempo or sound, The Treasury Records exists to support artists who push the boundaries of hard electronic music, shifting styles while keeping the energy high. It’s about building a solid foundation for unique, high quality music and the community that drives it.
